About

I am a third-year PhD student advised by Dr. Qiang Guan at Kent State University and Dr. Feixiong Cheng at Cleveland Clinic.

I design quantum algorithms and systems for structural biology and drug discovery, and run them on IBM utility-scale quantum processors, including the one at Cleveland Clinic.

Research

My research aims to understand and design quantum computing methods that turn the physical properties of quantum systems into programmable, scalable, and verifiable information processing, building a new physical intelligence layer for biological information systems and drug discovery.
